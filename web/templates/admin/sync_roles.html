Authentik Role Synchronization

Synchronize competition Discord roles with Authentik groups and team seats.

  • Linked users get the Discord role for each of their Authentik groups (e.g., WCComps_GoldTeam); members linked to an active team get its team role and Blueteam
  • Anyone else holding one of those roles loses it, including members who haven't linked; they get it back once they link and are in the group or on an active team
  • Bots, and roles the bot can't manage, are left alone; problems are listed with the results
  • This also runs automatically every few minutes
