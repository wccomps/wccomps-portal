What to do: on Schools, press Import from CSV and upload a CSV with one row per school (school_name and contact_email, optionally secondary_email and notes). Choose the event the list is for: an event already set up in the active season, or New event with its name, date and type. Keep the mode on Replace the school list for the event's first list, then Upload and Preview, check the preview, and Confirm and Import.

What happens: the schools get teams 1 through N in random order, those teams are activated, every team name goes back to its default, and the event is opened. Only one event is open at a time.

A new school year: the import page names the season a new event goes in. At the start of a school year, choose New event and fill in New season name and New season year: the import creates the season, makes it the active season and puts the new event in it. If no season is active, the new season is required.

Late registrations: import a file with the new schools and choose Add schools to the open event. Schools already on the list keep their team numbers and are skipped; each new school gets a free team. This works after packets went out and after teams started using their numbers. Then send them the packet with Send to Teams Without It (step 2).

What can go wrong: the page refuses to Replace a list once packets were sent for the open event, or once teams started using their numbers (scores, tickets or Discord links), because new numbers would send logins to the wrong school. It says to use Add schools, or to change one school on its School Info page. It refuses a list for another event while one is still open, and says whether to close that one out or cancel it. If someone changed the active season while you had the page open, it asks you to reload.
