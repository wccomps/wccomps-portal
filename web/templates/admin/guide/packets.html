What to do: on Packets, press Upload New Packet, open the packet, and press Distribute.

What happens: each school on the list gets the packet and its team's username and password (the password is set the first time the team is sent a packet). The packet is emailed if Email was ticked when it was uploaded, and teams can download it in the portal unless that was turned off. The packet then belongs to the open event.

Late teams and failed emails: after Add schools, open the sent packet and press Send to Teams Without It: it sends to the teams on the list that have no copy yet. Resend Failed and Retry Pending cover emails that failed or were interrupted. The Event panel notes the draft packets not sent and the teams on the list without a delivered copy.

What can go wrong: a packet can't be sent while no event is open (import the school list first). A packet sent for an earlier event can't be sent again, because team numbers changed since: upload it again as a new packet.
