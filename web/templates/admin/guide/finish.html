What to do: white team grades injects under White Team, and the reviews are approved under Scoring Review. Then Email Scorecards sends each school with a contact email its PDF scorecard, usually on the Sunday or Monday after.

What happens: grading usually takes a day or two after the competition. Scores are still live data, so they can change until Close Out. Once the scorecards are emailed, the event card moves on to Close Out Event. Sends made before the portal recorded them don't show, so the panel may still say no scorecard email is recorded. If you already emailed them, you can close out.

What the page tells you: once the competition is stopped, the Before you close out section of the Competition page, shown while this step or Close Out Event is next, checks for unfinished work: grades, tickets, incident reports, red team findings and orange scores not approved, whether the scorecards were emailed and still match the scores, and anything that would keep Close Out from saving a team's results. Each item links to where it is finished.
