What to do: press Close Out Event and type yolo to confirm.

What happens: in one step it saves every listed school's scorecard and a zip of every export as the event's results, then deletes the live data, closes the event, clears the schedule, the start message, the Quotient metadata and queued announcements, and queues the Discord cleanup (team channels, team roles and student helper roles). The school list stays until the next import. The results are kept in the portal; there is no page to view them yet. It can't be undone, so finish grading and email the scorecards first.

Scores with no event: if teams have scores but no event is open (an event ran without an import), Close Out asks for the event's name, date and type and records the scores at a new event in the active season, which it names. It needs an active season: if none is active, make one active on the Seasons page first.

What can go wrong: it refuses while the competition runs (stop it first), when no team has scores (cancel the event instead), and when a score would be lost: a team on the list that is inactive, or a team with scores but no school. The message names the teams and links to where each is fixed (Activate Team, the team's School Info page, or removing its school when no packets went out).
