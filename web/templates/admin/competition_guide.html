This page walks through one event, from the school list to the season's results. You don't need to remember it: the Event panel at the top of the Competition page always says where the event stands, the one next step, and links to where it is done.

Live data means everything the portal holds for the event in progress: scores, inject grades, red team findings, incident reports, orange assignments, tickets and the blue teams' Discord links. It stays until Close Out Event (or Clear leftover data) deletes it, so grading can go on after the competition stops.

1. Import the school list

Where: Schools, then Import from CSV.

What to do: upload a CSV with one row per school (school_name and contact_email, optionally secondary_email and notes). Choose the event the list is for: an event already set up in the active season, or New event with its name, date and type. Keep the mode on Replace the school list for the event's first list, then Upload and Preview, check the preview, and Confirm and Import.

What happens: the schools get teams 1 through N in random order, those teams are activated, every team name goes back to its default, and the event is opened. Only one event is open at a time.

A new school year: the import page names the season the event will be in ("This event will be in season ..."). At the start of a school year, choose New event and fill in Start a new season (a name and a year): the import creates the season, makes it the active season and puts the new event in it. If no season is active, the new season is required.

Late registrations: import a file with the new schools and choose Add schools to the open event. Schools already on the list keep their team numbers and are skipped; each new school gets a free team. This works after packets went out and after teams started using their numbers. Then send them the packet with Send to Teams Without It (step 2).

What can go wrong: the page refuses to Replace a list once packets were sent for the open event, or once teams started using their numbers (scores, tickets or Discord links), because new numbers would send logins to the wrong school. It says to use Add schools, or to change one school on its School Info page. It refuses a list for another event while one is still open, and says whether to close that one out or cancel it.

2. Send packets

Where: Packets. Upload New Packet, open it, and press Distribute.

What happens: each school on the list is emailed the packet with its team's username and password (the password is set the first time the team is sent a packet), and, unless the packet turns it off, teams can download it in the portal. The packet then belongs to the open event.

Late teams: after Add schools, open the sent packet and press Send to Teams Without It. It sends to the teams on the list that have no copy yet. Resend Failed and Retry Pending cover sends that failed or were interrupted.

What can go wrong: a packet can't be sent while no event is open (import the school list first). A packet sent for an earlier event can't be sent again, because team numbers changed since: upload it again as a new packet. The Event panel shows how many draft packets are not sent and how many teams on the list have no copy of a sent packet.

3. Check readiness

Where: Readiness Check on the Competition page. Press Run Checks.

What happens: it checks that the team accounts exist and are in the right groups, the Discord settings are set, the packets went out, the start message is set, Quotient answers and is synced, and no tickets, scores, grades or orange assignments are left from before. Some results have a button that fixes them or a link to where they are fixed.

Before competition day, also set up the Competition page: Max Team Members and the Controlled Apps (the apps Start and Stop open and close), the Schedule, the Start Message (posted to every active team when the competition starts) and Sync Metadata under Quotient Integration.

4. Start the competition

Where: Competition Controls, Start Competition. If a start time is set under Schedule, the bot starts it at that time on its own.

What happens: the controlled apps open to blue teams, the active teams' accounts are turned on, Quotient metadata is synced, and the start message is posted to every active team.

What can go wrong: with no controlled apps configured it refuses. If some apps or accounts didn't change, the progress list marks them and says the run is incomplete: run Start again.

5. Stop the competition

Where: Competition Controls, Stop Competition, or the end time under Schedule.

What happens: student access goes off: the controlled apps close and every team account is turned off. The live data stays, so grading goes on. You can start again later.

6. Finish scoring and email scorecards

Where: white team grades injects under White Team, and the reviews are approved under Scoring Review. Then Email Scorecards sends each school its scorecard.

What happens: grading usually takes a day or two after the competition. Scores are still live data, so they can change until Close Out. Email Scorecards emails each school with a contact email its PDF scorecard, usually on the Sunday or Monday after.

What the page tells you: once the competition is stopped, the Event panel and the Close Out Event section check for unfinished work: grades, tickets, incident reports, red team findings and orange scores not approved, and whether the scorecards were emailed and still match the scores. Each item links to where it is finished.

7. Close Out Event

Where: Close Out Event on the Competition page. Type yolo to confirm.

What happens: in one step it freezes every listed school's scorecard and a zip of every export as the event's results for the season, then deletes the live data, closes the event, clears the schedule, the start message, the Quotient box list and queued announcements, and queues the Discord cleanup (team channels, roles and helpers). The school list stays until the next import. It can't be undone, so finish grading and email the scorecards first.

Scores with no event: if teams have scores but no event is open (an event ran without an import), Close Out asks for the event's name, date and type and records the scores at a new event in the active season, which it names (or in a new season it asks for, when none is active).

What can go wrong: it refuses while the competition runs (stop it first), when no team has scores (cancel the event instead), and when a score would be lost: a school on the list whose team is inactive, or a team with scores but no school. The message says which teams and what to fix.

Rare: Cancel event

Where: Danger Zone, under Advanced on the Competition page.

For an event opened by mistake, or called off before any team has scores. It closes the event and removes its team assignments, so it can be deleted; nothing is recorded for it. The school list and sent packets stay. Discord links and tickets are then left over for Clear leftover data. Once teams have scores, close the event out instead.

Rare: Clear leftover data

Where: Danger Zone.

When no event is open but live data is still there (for example tickets or Discord links after Cancel event), the Event panel says so and links here. Clearing deletes that data for good; it is not archived. Results of events already closed out are not touched. If the leftovers include scores that were never recorded and no event was ever closed out, the page asks you to record them with Close Out Event instead.
