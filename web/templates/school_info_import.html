Upload a CSV file with one row per school. Replace the school list replaces all existing school information, assigns the schools to teams 1 through N in random order, activates those teams, and opens the chosen event.

Add schools keeps the open event's school list as it is and gives the file's new schools free teams (the lowest free team numbers, in random order), activating them. Schools already on the list keep their team numbers and are skipped, so it works after packets went out and teams started using their numbers.

CSV Format Requirements

Your CSV file must include the following columns:

  • school_name (required) - Name of the school or organization
  • contact_email (required) - Primary contact email address
  • secondary_email (optional) - Secondary contact email address
  • notes (optional) - Additional notes about the team
